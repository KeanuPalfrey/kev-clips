Kev Clips

A private desktop companion that turns Twitch stream clips into vertical videos and posts them to the streamer's own social accounts.

Kev Clips is used only by streamer Mev Bushwacker (MevBushwacker on Twitch) on his own Windows PC in Queensland, Australia. It is not a public or multi-user product.

The app creates clips from his Twitch streams, converts them into vertical 9:16 videos with subtitles and titles, and uploads or posts them to his own accounts on TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram, Facebook Reels and X.

TikTok integration uses TikTok for Developers Login Kit and the Content Posting API. The streamer connects his own TikTok account so the app can show the connected profile, send videos to TikTok drafts, or publish them directly when that option is enabled.

Tokens and app data are stored locally on the PC only. There is no Kev Clips server, and no data is sold, shared for advertising, or used for analytics.
