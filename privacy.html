Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how Kev Clips ("the app", "we", "us") handles information. Kev Clips is a private desktop companion operated by Mev Bushwacker from Queensland, Australia. It is used only by the streamer on his own Windows PC. It is not a public multi-user service and it does not run a backend server that collects personal information.

2. Information the app accesses

Depending on the features you use, the app may access:

  • TikTok basic profile information, obtained through Login Kit and the user.info.basic scope: a TikTok open id, display name and avatar, used to show the connected account in the app.
  • TikTok access tokens and refresh tokens, so the app can stay connected and upload or publish videos you choose to send.
  • Twitch clip and video data, such as clip identifiers, titles, source video and related stream metadata needed to create and convert a clip.
  • Videos, titles, captions, hashtags and other content you create or edit in the app, including the finished 9:16 video file.
  • Local app settings, such as your posting preferences and which platforms you have connected.

The app does not ask for your TikTok password. Sign-in happens on TikTok's own pages.

3. How the information is used

Information is used only to operate the app on your computer:

  • To confirm which TikTok account is connected, using the display name and avatar.
  • To create clips, convert them into vertical videos with subtitles and titles, and prepare captions.
  • To upload a video to your TikTok drafts or inbox (video.upload), or to publish it to your TikTok account (video.publish), when you choose that action.
  • To post to other platforms you connect and select, such as YouTube Shorts, Instagram, Facebook Reels and X.

We do not use this information for advertising, profiling, analytics, resale or any purpose unrelated to posting your own content.

4. Storage

Tokens, settings, clip files and related data are stored locally on the user's PC only. Kev Clips does not operate a server that receives, stores or processes this information on our behalf.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share your information with third parties for advertising or marketing. Data leaves your computer only when you choose to post or upload, and only to the platforms you select for that post. Those platforms then handle the content under their own terms and privacy policies.

We may disclose information if required by law, but because the app stores data only on your device, we do not hold a copy on a Kev Clips server.

6. Retention and deletion

Local data is kept on your computer for as long as you use the app or until you delete it. You can disconnect TikTok in the app, which stops further TikTok access and removes or invalidates the stored TikTok tokens. Deleting the app folder from your computer deletes all local Kev Clips data, including tokens, settings and locally stored videos created by the app.

Content you have already posted on TikTok or another platform remains on that platform until you delete it there. Their retention practices are controlled by those services, not by Kev Clips.

7. Security

The app stores tokens and files on your local computer and does not transmit them to a Kev Clips server. You are responsible for the security of your PC, user account and backups. No method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security of data stored on your device.

8. Children

The Service is a private desktop tool for an adult streamer. It is not directed at children under 13, and it is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your rights

Because information is stored on your own computer, you can access, correct or delete it by using the app's settings, disconnecting a platform, or deleting the app folder. If Australian privacy law or other applicable law gives you additional rights, you can contact us using the details below and we will help where we can. Please allow a reasonable time for a response.

10. Revoking TikTok access

You can disconnect TikTok at any time in the Kev Clips app. You can also revoke the app's access in your TikTok account: go to Settings and privacy > Security > Apps and services (or the equivalent manage app permissions screen) and remove Kev Clips. After access is revoked, the app will no longer be able to read your basic TikTok profile or upload or publish videos to TikTok until you connect again.

11. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.

12. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy or about personal information can be sent to mev_bushwacker@outlook.com.
